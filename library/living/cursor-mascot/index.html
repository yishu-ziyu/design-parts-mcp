/page-mascot

Skill to generate a character for the top of your page. It follows the reader’s cursor and blinks when they poke it.

Use one of the 53GitHub

Pick one below, or have an agent skill draw a new one.

Different styles

You can pick from one of the six styles. Or send a photo and get yourself turned into a character.

53 already drawn

All of them follow your cursor. Poke one, or hover it and press Use to find out how to put it on your page.

可拆

#hero
两张 3×3 精灵图叠放：跟随鼠标时换 background-position，不做过渡；被点时切到表情图并压扁回弹。
#styles
同一角色的七种画法并排，悬停出黑色 Use 胶囊。
#grid
六列角色墙，每个都独立看向鼠标。
#panel
右侧 620px 抽屉，220ms 滑入，步骤编号加深色代码块。
