What is it that makes things beautiful? A perceptible excess of consideration. Our environment used to be coated with little surpluses of consideration—like wiggles and floral motifs in lamp posts and public fountains. All these small things contribute to an ambient atmosphere of beauty; and, more importantly, they announce that there is no thing too menial to deserve aesthetic refinement. Alembic's mission is this, but applied to ideas: there is no thought too menial to be worth a considered presentation.
